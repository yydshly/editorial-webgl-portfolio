# Release positioning and external statement

## Locked identity

> This is a fictional character-brand and DOM-first WebGL spatial-narrative
> experiment. DEV-HOST-01 is a fictional development identity. Its timeline is
> fictional, and the three FIELD NOTES books are concept publications created
> for the experiment. The project does not provide real purchasing, ticketing,
> publishing, awards, or person-related commercial services.

Use this wording, or a shorter equivalent preserving every factual guard, in
README, About/footer development disclosure, social-share description, and
release notes. Do not imply that DEV-HOST-01 is a real person.

## Short forms

README/release note: `DEV-HOST-01 is a fictional development archive: a
DOM-first WebGL spatial narrative experiment with concept publications and
development imagery. It is not a real-person, publishing, ticketing,
purchasing, or commercial service.`

Social share: `DEV-HOST-01 - a fictional development editorial archive and
DOM-first WebGL storytelling experiment.`

## Two release levels

### Demo RC

The Demo RC is a noindex, nofollow, controlled demonstration. It may use
development AI imagery and concept covers when the fictional disclosure is
present and no real commercial flow is implied. Local development, private
preview links, and password-protected reviews are allowed. A public noindex
portfolio URL requires a separate release-owner decision.

### Public Production

Public Production requires approved identity/domain, licensed assets, source
and commercial-use evidence, releases, production OG art, signed art review,
approved privacy/copyright/disclosure text, robots/index approval, and named
hosting, monitoring, rollback, release, and emergency-offline owners. Until
these gates are complete, this is not a public production release.
